Our Story

Bridging Factories to Structural Foundations

Founded in 2012, BuildPro emerged from a fundamental challenge in industrial construction: procurement friction. Core materials (steel rebars, Portland cement, vitrified blocks) were bottlenecked by layered distributors, lacking testing certificates, and experiencing chronic site transport delays.

We resolved this by rebuilding the pipeline digitally. By negotiating direct supply agreements with Tier-1 manufacturers and integrating automated laboratory batch testing, we created an open portal where contractors can secure volume discounts with 100% QA transparency.

Ethos & Foundations

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Our operational compass guiding procurement actions and logistics delivery daily.

Our Mission

To eliminate logistics downtime and procurement markups in heavy construction supply chains by providing direct-import, laboratory-verified building materials digitally.

Our Vision

To be the default material pipeline for civil infrastructure globally, setting the industry benchmark for carbon-compliant green cement and smart logistics routing.

Structural Integrity

We prioritize physical material safety. Compressive capacity, steel ductility, and glass thickness reports are absolute standards before shipping offloads.

History Timeline

Our Growth Milestones

Timeline of innovation and fleet expansion from 2012 to today.

2012

BuildPro Foundations

Launched in Cleveland with 3 concrete transit mixers, supplying direct-import cement to local residential developers.

2016

Fleet Expansion & Steel Contracts

Signed direct wholesale sourcing agreements with structural steel rolling mills. Added flatbeds and flatbed trailers to owned fleet.

2020

Launch of Digital Procurement Hub

Rolled out the online contractor portal. Enabled real-time GPS tracking and instant test certificate downloads.

2024

Green Materials Initiative

Began supply lines for pozzolana eco-cement (PPC) and low carbon emission bricks to support green building classifications.
