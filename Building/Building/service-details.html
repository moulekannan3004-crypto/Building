Operational Advantages

Structured Sourcing Fleet Benefits

Standard freight forwarders rarely understand heavy construction layouts. They offload structural steel or palletized bricks at outer fences, causing secondary haulage costs. BuildPro's dedicated trucks are operated by experienced layout drivers.

  • On-Site Placement & Offloading: Every flatbed truck carrying blocks or steel is equipped with on-board hydraulic cranes (Hiab loaders) to offload cargo directly inside laydown zones.
  • Transit Mixing Concrete Delivery: Dispatch transit concrete mixers with integrated retarder additives to prevent premature drying. Coordinates perfectly with site pumps.
  • Active GPS Telematics: Receive automated SMS notifications with a tracking link when your shipment is within 15 miles of the site gate, ensuring layout preparation.
